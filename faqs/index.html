A few answers to common questions below.

ORDERS & SHIPPING

How long will my order take to get here?

Orders leave the studio within 2–3 business days. Carriers estimate 3–5 business days after that, though 5–10 days is more realistic once everything settles. You'll get a tracking number as soon as your order ships.

Full details: Shipping & Returns

Where's my order?

You'll get a tracking number by email as soon as your order ships. If it's taking noticeably longer than expected, email hello@knocktwice.studio with your order number and we'll look into it.

Do you ship outside the US?

Not right now. If you're outside the US and really love the products, email us — we can't promise anything, but we'll see what's possible.

My order arrived damaged. Now what?

Email hello@knocktwice.studio within 7 days of delivery with your order number and a few photos of the damage. You can choose either a replacement or a full refund, including any shipping you paid.

RETURNS & REFUNDS

Can I return something?

Yes, within 14 days of delivery. The product needs to be unused and in its original packaging, and return shipping is on you.

Full details: Shipping & Returns

When will I see my refund?

We process refunds within 5–7 business days of your return arriving. After that, your bank can take another 5–10 business days to show it on your statement.

Can I exchange a product for a different one?

We don't do direct exchanges right now. Return the one you have for a refund and place a new order.

THE PRODUCTS THEMSELVES

The color looks a little different than on the site. Is something wrong?

Probably not. Monitors, phones, and lighting all render color differently, and material and finish vary from product to product. If something genuinely doesn't match what we showed, email us and we'll make it right.

Do you make custom sizes?

Email hello@knocktwice.studio and tell us what you have in mind.
